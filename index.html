Overprint

Two sets of fine rings meet off-centre. Follow the overlap to see a third pattern appear.

Details ⌄
Try tracing one violet ring with your finger. The tighter bands form where the two sets cross.

Overprint exercise

Make two copies of one ring pattern on tracing paper. Slide the top sheet sideways until the overlap seems to vibrate.

After Hours

A broken spiral follows the path of a late walk back from the studio.

Details ⌄
The spiral has an opening on one side. Each turn moves that opening a little farther around the page.

After Hours exercise

Sketch the same walk three times without lifting your pencil. Keep the version that best captures where you slowed down.

Fold / Form

Six tapered shapes turn a flat sheet into a study of light and folded edges.

Details ⌄
The cream wedges read like lit faces while the rust wedges suggest the reverse side of a fold.

Fold / Form exercise

Fold a scrap sheet into a fan and stand it beside a window. Draw the bright faces first, then fill in the shadows.

Signal

A stack of blue waves stretches a single sound across the width of a page.

Details ⌄
The rows shift slightly out of step, so the crest travels diagonally down the sheet.

Signal exercise

Hum one note, then sketch its rhythm as a line. Repeat the line ten times and shift each crest slightly.

Soft Type

Uppercase and lowercase letters share the frame at two very different scales.

Details ⌄
Look at the enclosed spaces inside the letters. Changing their size changes how much background shows through.

Soft Type exercise

Choose two letters from your name. Enlarge one until it touches the margin, then fit the smaller one into the space it leaves.

Margin Notes

Uneven rules turn a page of imagined annotations into a quiet block of colour.

Details ⌄
The bars share a left margin. Their unequal lengths suggest handwriting seen from too far away to read.

Margin Notes exercise

Pick a paragraph from your notebook and replace every line with a bar of the same length. Keep the original line breaks.
