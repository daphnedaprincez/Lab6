Lotus in Bloom

A pink lotus rises above the water, its layered petals echoing the soft pink lettering at the top of the print.

Details ⌄
Two smaller blooms frame the Chinese title 蓮花 at the bottom. Together they mirror the large flower above and draw the eye back to the centre of the poster.

About Lotus in Bloom

The text connects the lotus with purity and awakening in Buddhism. The illustration gives that idea a visible form: a long stem lifts the open flower into the light above the pond.

Wisteria in Violet

Pale lavender blossoms give way to deep violet buds along three hanging clusters of wisteria.

Details ⌄
The tallest flower cluster overlaps the WISTERIA lettering, while a pointed leaf extends past the lower edge of the lavender panel.

About Wisteria in Violet

Small wisteria motifs repeat in all four corners, framing the central illustration.

Botanical Fair

A bouquet of golden flowers spills beyond a sky-blue frame on a textured cream background.

Details ⌄
A tiny blue flower sits inside the O of FLOWER. Beneath it, the looping yellow letters of “grow” pick up the colour of the petals.

About Botanical Fair

The bouquet stretches from the headline to the bottom edge of the print. Around it, the repeated “Botanical Fair” lettering and the Columbia Road location identify the event. The artwork carries the signature “Lara Cunha Designs” in the lower right.

Lavender in Lilac

Lilac blossoms range from deep violet to pale lavender, with the tallest cluster reaching into the title.

Details ⌄
Look closely at the word LILAC. Tiny floral outlines fill each letter, echoing the individual blossoms in the illustration below.

About Lavender in Lilac

The left margin identifies the flower as Syringa vulgaris. Across from it, Japanese lettering sits above a short poem. Together, these details give the print the feel of a page from an illustrated botanical collection.

Hydrangeas in the Rain

Raindrops bead on dark leaves beneath clusters of hydrangeas.

Details ⌄
Look between the two flower heads on the left to find the iron garden gate. Its fine blue bars fade into the background, making the pale petals in front stand out.

About Hydrangeas in the Rain

The poster names the flower in three places. HYDRANGEA stretches across the top and a small botanical label identifies it as Hydrangea macrophylla. A handwritten line about rain beneath the title sets the mood for the garden scene.

Margin Notes

Details ⌄
The bars share a left margin. Their unequal lengths suggest handwriting seen from too far away to read.
